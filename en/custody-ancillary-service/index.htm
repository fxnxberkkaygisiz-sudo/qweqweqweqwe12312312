    Custody & Ancillary Service

    Open an investment account with Körfez Menkul to trade in the financial markets and securely hold your capital market instruments through our custody services.

    Custody Services in Financial Markets

    Intermediary institutions authorized to trade in capital markets can also provide custody services alongside brokerage transactions. Custody services can be briefly defined as the safekeeping of capital market instruments on behalf of the customer. This service includes the safekeeping of financial instruments subject to brokerage as well as managed portfolios. The products that can be subject to custody service can be listed as capital market instruments such as stocks, money market instruments, cash, foreign currency, deposits, precious metals, futures, and options.

    In addition to protecting financial assets, custody service also covers basic functions such as managing records, verifying the existence of assets, and controlling asset movements. This service also includes ancillary services such as the payment of dividends and capital increases in share transactions, payment of tax refunds from transactions, and regular information provision to investors. Custody services in capital markets can be divided into three categories based on their scope: limited custody, general custody, and portfolio custody.

    Limited Custody Service

    Limited custody service is restricted to the safekeeping of capital market instruments and individual portfolios that the authorized institutions provide brokerage services for and have authority over. Capital market instruments intermediated for public offerings can also be stored within the scope of the limited custody service.

    General Custody Service

    General custody service can be defined as a custody service where authorized institutions have the authority to reliably store, manage, and trade the financial assets of investors. Instruments such as domestic and foreign stocks, sukuk, i.e. lease certificates, investment funds, government bonds, private sector bonds, treasury bills may be included in the scope of general custody service.

    Unlike the limited custody services, authorized institutions are a service that is independent of the instruments that are intermediary and authorized. Under the general custody service, provided by generally authorized institutions, assets transferred by investors from different institution via transfer can also be stored. Central custody services;. In addition to safekeeping and keeping records of assets, it may also include services such as carrying out operational transactions and tax obligations.

    Portfolio Custody Service

    Portfolio custody services encompass the secure safekeeping, recordkeeping, and oversight of investors’ financial portfolios. In addition to transactions such as asset purchases and sales and the collection of dividend income, these services offer benefits including portfolio analysis and performance monitoring.
     
    Through technological integration, portfolio custody services consolidate investors’ financial assets and facilitate transactions. Automated reporting enables investors to monitor their assets from a single point, making the management of financial transactions both secure and convenient.
     
    In addition to holding General Custody authorization and a Portfolio Custody licence, Körfez Menkul provides custody services for investors’ portfolios, as well as Collective Portfolio Custody services for Securities Investment Funds, Venture Capital Investment Funds, and Real Estate Investment Funds. Within the scope of these services, Körfez Menkul Değerler A.Ş. manages all oversight and operational processes relating to the relevant funds.
     
    As a fully authorized investment institution, Körfez Menkul provides reliable services for the custody of your capital market instruments and institutional portfolios, as well as the fulfilment of related operational and tax obligations.

    • What do Körfez Menkul’s custody services cover?

      Körfez Menkul’s custody services involve the secure safekeeping of capital market instruments on behalf of clients. These services cover financial instruments such as equities, money market instruments, cash, foreign currencies, deposits, precious metals, futures and options.

      In addition to safeguarding assets, the services include record management, verification of asset existence, monitoring of asset movements, processing dividend and capital increase payments, processing tax refunds arising from transactions, and providing investors with regular updates.

    • How are custody fees determined for investment funds?

      Custody costs for investment funds vary depending on the criteria established by custody service providers and the types of assets held by the fund. In general, the cost of custody services is calculated based on the fund’s size, transaction volume, and the nature of the assets held in custody. Depending on the terms specified in the custody service agreement, the costs charged to the investor may affect the fund’s returns.

    • How does Körfez Menkul’s portfolio custody service work?

      Körfez Menkul’s portfolio custody service involves the secure safekeeping, recordkeeping, and monitoring of investors’ financial portfolios. In addition to covering transactions such as asset purchases and sales and the collection of dividend income, the custody service offers benefits such as portfolio analysis and performance tracking.

      Through technological integration, the service enables investors to keep all their financial assets in one place, simplifying transactions and allowing them to monitor their assets from a single point. This makes the management of financial transactions more secure and convenient.
