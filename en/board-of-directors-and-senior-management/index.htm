    Dr. R. Ahmet ALBAYRAK

    Dr. R. Ahmet ALBAYRAK

    Born in Istanbul in 1966, Ahmet Albayrak graduated from the Industrial Engineering department of Istanbul Technical University in 1988. He received a master's degree in Organizational Leadership and
    Business Administration from North Carolina State University (USA) in 1993. In 2007, he received the title of Doctor at Istanbul Technical University for his work on Technology Management.
    Albayrak began his banking career as a Specialist at Albaraka Türk Participation Bank Inc. in 1988. He joined Kuveyt Türk in 1994, where he worked in the Financial Analysis and Marketing departments until 1996. From 1996 to 2001, he held senior management positions in the private sector. In 2002, he rejoined Kuveyt Türk as the Deputy General Manager responsible for Branches. In 2005, he was appointed Deputy General Manager for Operations, Technology, and Administrative Services. With the reorganization in 2008, he also oversaw the Human Resources, Training and Development, Quality, and Strategy Monitoring departments under the Banking Service Group. As of October 2012, he continued his role as Deputy General Manager for Treasury and International Banking at Kuveyt Türk. As of 2024, Ahmet Albayrak has been appointed Chairman of the Board of Körfez Menkul.

    Aslan DEMİR

    Aslan DEMİR

    Born in Amasya in 1971, Aslan Demir graduated from Marmara University with a degree in International Relations. He completed an Executive MBA program at the University of Sheffield.
    Demir began his banking career in 1995 at Kuveyt Türk as a Treasury Manager, where he served for six years. From 2001 to 2004, he continued his career in Project Management and Quality Management. In 2005, he was appointed as the Project Management and Quality Manager and continued his career as the Information Technologies Group Manager following the reorganization in 2007. As of October 2012, he has been working as Deputy General Manager responsible for Strategy at Kuveyt Türk. In 2024, Aslan Demir was appointed as a Board Member of Körfez Menkul.

    Abdurrahman DELİPOYRAZ

    Abdurrahman DELİPOYRAZ

    Born in Istanbul in 1968, Abdurrahman Delipoyraz graduated from Istanbul Technical University with a degree in Industrial Engineering. He began his career at Kuveyt Türk in the Project and Investment Department and worked in the Corporate and Commercial Banking Sales Department as well as the Financial Analysis and Intelligence Department until 2000.
    From 2000 to 2004, Delipoyraz held branch management positions at Beşyüzevler and Bakırköy branches. In December 2004, he was appointed as a Regional Manager, serving in the Istanbul European-1 and Istanbul Anatolian Regional Directorates. Since January 2015, Delipoyraz has been the Deputy General Manager responsible for Sme Banking at Kuveyt Türk. In 2024, he was appointed as a Board Member of Körfez Menkul.

    Bilal SAYIN

    Bilal SAYIN

    Born in Sakarya in 1966, Bilal Sayın graduated from Middle East Technical University (METU) with a degree in Public Administration in 1990. He began his banking career at Albaraka Türk in 1990 and continued at Kuveyt Türk in the Project and Investment Department in 1995.
    In 1999, Sayın was appointed as the Department Head of Corporate and Commercial Loans at Kuveyt Türk. Since 2003, he has served as the Deputy General Manager responsible for Loans at Kuveyt Türk. As of 2024, Bilal Sayın has been appointed as a Board Member of Körfez Menkul.

    Dr. Selman ORTAKÖY

    Dr. Selman ORTAKÖY

    Born in Amasya in 1984, Dr. Selman Ortaköy graduated from Bogazici University with a degree in Economics in 2007. He completed his master's degree in Economics and Finance at Bahçeşehir University in 2009 and earned his doctorate in Economics from Istanbul University in 2014.
    Ortaköy began his career at Kuveyt Türk in 2007 as a Business Banking Marketing Specialist. From 2009 to 2013, he served as the Manager of Corporate Performance Management in the Audit
    Committee. In 2017, he was appointed as the Operations Group Manager and continued his career as the Strategy and Innovation Group Manager from 2020 to 2023.
    As of 2024, Dr. Selman Ortaköy has been appointed as the General Manager and Board Member of Körfez Menkul.

    Turgay ÇAVAŞ

    Turgay ÇAVAŞ

    Eşref Said ÜNLÜ

    Eşref Said ÜNLÜ

    Esma TEZCAN

    Esma TEZCAN

    Esma Tezcan was born in Istanbul in 1990 and graduated from Marmara University, Department of Business Administration (German) in 2014. She started his banking career in 2014 with Körfez Menkul Bank Inc. She started as an Assistant Specialist in the Individual Marketing Department, Securities and Insurance Service.

    Dr. Kutay GÖZGÖR

    Dr. Kutay GÖZGÖR

    Born in Kadıköy in 1988, Dr. Kutay Gözgör graduated from Doğuş University with a degree in Economics in 2012. He completed his master’s degree in Financial Markets and Investment Management at Marmara University in 2014, and earned his doctorate in Economics from Gebze Technical University in 2023.

    Abdulkadir SADIKOĞLU

    Abdulkadir SADIKOĞLU

    Born in Konya in 1983, Abdulkadir Sadıkoğlu graduated from Boğaziçi University with a degree in Economics in 2008. He began his professional career in 2009 as an Assistant Specialist at the Capital Markets Board, Inspection Department. He later served as a Specialist and Senior Specialist in the Market Surveillance and Inspection Department and the Corporate Finance Department.
    From 2013 to 2017, he represented Turkey on the Committee on Secondary Markets at the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO). In 2019, he obtained a Master’s degree in Finance (MiF) from London Business School (LBS).
    As of 2024, Abdulkadir Sadıkoğlu has been appointed as the Corporate Finance Director at Körfez Menkul Inc., where he continues his duties.

    Dr. Mehmet ODABAŞI

    Dr. Mehmet ODABAŞI

    Born in Sivas in 1975, Mehmet Odabaşı graduated from the Faculty of Theology at Marmara University in 1998. Following his undergraduate studies, he completed his master's degree in Islamic Law at the Institute of Social Sciences of the same university. Continuing his academic studies in Islamic Law, he completed his PhD program at Marmara University's Institute of Social Sciences in June 2026 and earned his doctoral degree.

    Between 2004 and 2016, he held various positions within the Advisory Committee of Kuveyt Türk Participation Bank Inc. In 2016, he was appointed as a member of the Advisory Committee of Kuveyt Türk Participation Bank Inc.

    In addition to his role at our company, Odabaşı currently serves as a member of the Advisory Committees of KT Bank, KT Portföy, and Katılım Emeklilik.

    Odabaşı also contributed to the project undertaken to translate the work Al-Ma'ayir Al-Shar'iyyah, published by the Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (AAOIFI), into Turkish under the title Faizsiz Finans Standartları (Interest-Free Finance Standards). The project was supported by the Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BRSA), sponsored by the Participation Banks Association of Türkiye (TKBB), and partially undertaken by Istanbul Sabahattin Zaim University. Odabaşı contributed to the project by translating half of the publication.

    A father of three, Odabaşı continues his academic and practical work in the fields of interest-free banking and insurance.
