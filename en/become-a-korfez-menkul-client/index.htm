    How Can You Become a Körfez Menkul Client?

    • 1

      Download Körfez Mobile and tap the “I Want to Become a Customer” button.

      Detailed Instructions

      • 1a. Complete the application form. 
      • 1b. Scan the back of your chip-enabled ID card using your camera. 
      • 1c. Hold your ID card close to your phone to allow its information to be read via NFC. 
      • 1d. Position your face in front of the camera as shown on the screen. 
      • 1e. Start the video call and speak with our customer representative. 
      • 1f. You have successfully completed your application to become a Kuveyt Türk customer. 
    • 2

      In Körfez Mobile, follow these steps: Foreign Exchange/Investments > My Investment Accounts > Open an Investment Account.

    • 3

      You can open your investment account by reviewing and accepting the relevant agreements.

    • 4

      You have successfully completed your application to become a Körfez Menkul client.

    • 5

      You can download the Körfez Menkul Mobile app and continue with your transactions.
