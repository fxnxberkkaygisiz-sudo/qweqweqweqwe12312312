    • Discover Körfez Menkul
    Körfez Menkul brings together a broad range of services — from equities, investment funds and sukuk to IPO underwriting and corporate finance — and delivers them to investors in line with participation finance principles.

    Körfez Menkul Değerler A.Ş. offers individual and corporate investors equity brokerage on the Borsa Istanbul equity market, investment fund and sukuk (lease certificate) transactions, and investment advisory services. It aims to provide a transparent, reliable and easily accessible investment experience for those who wish to grow their savings through interest-free instruments aligned with participation finance principles.

    On the corporate side, Körfez Menkul provides strategic and financial support to companies in IPO underwriting, sukuk issuances, corporate finance, company valuation, and mergers and acquisitions, delivering financing solutions tailored to their growth plans with an experienced team.

    With Körfez Menkul Mobile, the web platform and the online branch, investors can follow markets and their portfolios in real time and carry out their transactions securely from wherever they are. An investment account can be opened quickly and digitally through a video call.

    Operating within the framework of the Capital Markets Board of Türkiye regulations, Körfez Menkul aims to help its investors reach their financial goals through its customer-focused approach, strong technology infrastructure and innovative products.
